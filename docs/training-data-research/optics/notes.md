# Practical optical PSFs for JSR synthetic training

Research checked on 2026-10-05. This is a source-based proposal, not an installed or benchmarked optical simulator. No repository source/configuration changes, packages, large datasets, or worktrees were created. Small source pages and notes are retained beside this file.

## Recommendation

Use a hybrid bank: public, explicitly identified patent-example prescriptions for physical augmentation; ray-wave diffraction at calibrated representative states; and measured effective camera PSFs for validating or fitting each real camera/lens pairing. Keep the current circular-pupil/Zernike generator as labelled generic augmentation. Neither generic Zernike coefficients nor manufacturer MTF charts reconstruct the wavelength-resolved two-dimensional PSFs of a real production prime lens.

The best public implementation candidate verified here is KAUST's **DeepLens**. Its public source now contains geometric, exit-pupil diffraction, and Huygens models and several camera lens prescriptions. It supplies a practical route beyond the current assumed field law, but its filename labels, glass dispersion, field coverage, sampling, and CPU cost require validation before claiming a camera-specific physical simulation.

## Actual public prime prescriptions

[DeepLens camera directory](https://github.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/tree/main/datasets/lenses/camera) contains EF35/40/50/85/100, RF16/24/35/50, Sigma70, and Yongnuo50 files. All ten files beyond EF35 were individually fetched and parsed; results are in `lens_inventory.json`. Its [lens resource note](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/main/datasets/lenses/readme.md) says data were collected from PhotonsToPhotos OpticalBench and lens-designs.com. These are nominal/public design examples, not measured production-lens calibrations. Repository code licensing does not separately resolve redistribution rights for every third-party lens asset.

Good starting candidates, subject to recomputation and source verification:

- [EF50 file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/main/datasets/lenses/camera/ef50mm_f1.8.json): explicitly `JP 1987-087922 Example 1`, stored focal length 50.5204 mm, f-number 1.7867; 12 surfaces including aperture. It can supply a patent-example normal prime from which to stop down, after validating effective entrance pupil and focus.
- [EF35 file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/821498bc48415a14fec142276c967db1ac2fbcec/datasets/lenses/camera/ef35mm_f2.0.json): `JP 1992-015611 Example 1`, stored 34.8252 mm and f/2.3097. The filename's f/2 is not its computed/stored f-number.
- [RF35](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/main/datasets/lenses/camera/rf35mm_f1.8.json) and [RF50](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/main/datasets/lenses/camera/rf50mm_f1.8.json) explicitly identify US patent examples; stored focal lengths 36.0089 and 49.565 mm. Verify sensor geometry and missing default fields before export.
- [DiffOptics Nikon Z35 example](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DiffOptics/f62dc49aa45c5ea3f8165634622392fbe44e6448/examples/lenses/Zemax_samples/Nikon-z35-f1.8-JPA2019090949-example2.txt): says `Originate from Zemax, modified by Congli Wang`; filename identifies JPA2019090949 example 2. Total listed axial distance is 4.483, and the file declares no explicit unit/scale metadata. Confirm scale against the prescription before treating it as a 35 mm physical lens.

Two explicit reasons not to import the whole bank uncritically:

- `rf24mm_f1.8.json` identifies US 2022/0019061 example 3 but stores focal length **39.5385 mm** and f-number **5.2425**. These are observed file metadata, not a tested simulator defect: rederive optical properties from surfaces and compare the patent.
- `rf16mm_f2.8.json` says **“The original patent can not cover full-frame sensor.”** It stores 13.0533 mm/f2.3126. Check valid ray coverage at each field; normalization must not disguise vignetting or clipped bundles.

Most inspected camera materials are inline `nD/Abbe` values. [DeepLens materials implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/main/deeplens/material/materials.py) converts these to Cauchy approximation, although known catalog glasses can use Sellmeier/Schott/tabulated models. Two values do not establish an exact 400–700 nm production-glass dispersion curve. Coatings, element tolerances, decentering, blade geometry, focus-group motion, sensor cover glass, OLPF, and sample-to-sample variability remain calibration issues.

[DeepLens Zemax parser](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/main/deeplens/geolens_pkg/io.py) supports sequential STANDARD/EVENASPH surfaces and MM units and rejects unsupported surface types/coefficients. It is not a universal substitute for OpticStudio analysis. No licensed OpticStudio software or commercial sample collection was acquired. See `empirical/notes.md` for any independently verified official licensing/sample evidence.

## Measured alternatives and the licensed OpticStudio route

Additional primary-source checks, exact quotes and pinned revisions are in [empirical/notes.md](empirical/notes.md).

- [Delbracio psf-estim](https://github.com/mdelbra/psf-estim), [author README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mdelbra/psf-estim/master/README.txt): a registered/radiometrically corrected random Bernoulli target gives local subpixel, nonparametric PSFs by linear inversion. It explicitly estimates intrinsic camera blur due to **diffraction, sensor resolution, lens aberrations and anti-aliasing filters**. It provides calibration code/sample data, not a measured prime/spectral library. Code AGPLv3+; outputs are TXT float matrices.
- [Learning Lens Blur Fields](https://github.com/estherlin/learning-lens-blur-fields), [author README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/estherlin/learning-lens-blur-fields/main/README.md): official TPAMI 2025 acquisition/representation project. Released measured iPhone 12 Pro wide data are advertised as 1.06 GB downsampled, 18.3 GB full resolution, and 1.75 GB raw patterns. Inspected code predicts four RGGB channels with field/kernel/focus coordinates; it does not establish a 61-wavelength optical-only tensor with independent aperture/depth/focus. Code MIT; external data license unverified. README warns development code is uncleaned/unmaintained since 2023; some referenced paths are broken. Data link was blocked and no model was evaluated.
- [Eboli fast_two_stage_psf_correction](https://github.com/teboli/fast_two_stage_psf_correction), [author README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/teboli/fast_two_stage_psf_correction/main/README.md): ECCV 2022 official project, measured PSFs of dozens of lenses and a separate seven-parameter RGB Gaussian approximation. Its parser reads field/exposure/RGB MAT kernels with Canon EOS 5DSR dimensions and a filename `Canon_EF24mm_f_1.4L_USM_ap_1.4.mat`. This is a promising measured DSLR/prime collection for calibration or effective-kernel augmentation, but full lens/aperture/depth/spectral coverage and pixel-integration status were unverified: the external Edmond data endpoint was blocked. Code MIT; external dataset rights unverified.
- [Official Ansys programming library](https://github.com/ansys/lib-zemax-programming): verified pinned official README requires **an installed and licensed OpticStudio**. MIT examples reference installed Cooke/Double Gauss samples and create a transparent singlet; commercial sample prescription sharing rights are not established by that code license. Official source calls `New_HuygensPsf()` with 128² image/pupil sampling, but a numerical PSF-array export was not verified. This is a prospective licensed Windows exporter, not a cloud dependency already available. [Detailed sources and boundaries](empirical/zemax/notes.md).

Google Drive, Edmond, arXiv, publisher/university/Ansys documentation endpoints returned proxy CONNECT 403 during the delegated checks. These restrictions do not imply that the sources or datasets are absent. GitHub primary-source alternatives supplied the findings above; TLS was never disabled.

## Optical PSF versus an effective camera kernel

The current `load_psf_library` requires `contains_pixel_integration=false` and micrometer sampling equal to `pitch_um/scale`. An exported ray-wave intensity field must be sampled/integrated onto that **numerical reconstruction grid**, preserving a clearly defined optical-only model. Ray splatting and interpolation contribute numerical filtering; do not equate that with a measured native sensel aperture.

A point-source photograph or pattern-derived camera kernel normally includes pixel aperture, possible OLPF/cover-glass effects, source size/spectrum, and sometimes ISP/demosaicing. It cannot be truthfully marked optical-only just to satisfy the loader. Either fit an optical model **through** an explicit sensor/source calibration forward model, or later introduce a separate effective-kernel route that applies native integration exactly once. Optical recovery by naive division/deconvolution of pixel MTF is poorly constrained near its zeros and beyond sensor Nyquist.

Preserve each monochromatic kernel centroid relative to a common reference wavelength, so lateral chromatic displacement survives. Record distortion separately with the reference projection; independently recentering every wavelength would erase lateral color. Keep vignetting/throughput separately from unit-sum PSF shape. Blur belongs to sensor field coordinates after scene-motion warp. Validate the local four-corner interpolation against withheld field points; arbitrary production decentering need not obey radial symmetry.

## Practical staged bank and calibration

1. Audit a small set of public normal/wide/tele patent examples. Check units, effective focal length, entrance pupil/f-number, glass dispersion and supported image circle. Start with physically attainable stops f/2, 2.8, 4, 5.6, 8; never enlarge a nominal f/2.8 design to f/2 without identifying that extrapolation. Focus distance and object distance are separate state axes, not a single random defocus coefficient.
2. Precompute geometric field/wavelength/depth grids on CPU for coverage, distortion, centroids and rough aberration structure. Then use ray-wave reference PSFs at selected fields, wavelengths and stops; fit/refine the pupil or surrogate and adaptively add samples until withheld states pass. Interpolating sparse bands to all 61 wavelengths is allowed only with measured numerical error bounds; three RGB PSFs alone are insufficient evidence.
3. For representative real body/prime pairs, capture unclipped linear RAW calibration sequences: dark/flat correction, narrow-band illuminated point/pattern target, repeated exposures, subpixel target translations, multiple field positions, focus/object distance and actual aperture. Check projected source size and illumination bandwidth. Fit optical parameters through known source/sensel integration; reserve fields/wavelengths/states for validation. RGB-only measurements validate response-weighted effective RGB kernels, not individual 61-band optical PSFs.
4. Store provenance per bank: source/patent/revision, lens sample, stop and achieved f-number, sensor/cover-glass state, focus and object depth, wavelengths/bandwidth, physical sampling/orientation, reference centroids, throughput, retained support energy, source/pixel integration flags, and calibration uncertainty. Resample explicitly for each pitch/scale, then feed the existing external optical-PSF interface.
5. Train using physical-bank and generic augmentation distributions explicitly separated in metadata. Evaluate real held-out camera/lens pairs and partially saturated channels; synthetic PSF realism alone does not establish successful learned reconstruction.

## Cost and acceptance criteria

Verified [DeepLens defaults](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vccimaging/DeepLens/821498bc48415a14fec142276c967db1ac2fbcec/deeplens/config.py): geometric PSF 8192 rays; coherent PSF default **16777216 rays**. The coherent pupil code requires **float64 and at least 1000000 rays**, supports one source point, and propagates a sensor-height pupil grid with padding. Huygens has an explicit 10000-ray batching loop over `k*k` output samples, approximately `O(rays*k*k)` work, and is non-differentiable. GPU acceleration advertised as >10× in the README is an **in-house, unreleased extension**, not an available public setup promise.

For scale: 25 field nodes × 61 wavelengths × 5 stops = **7625 reference calls per lens/depth**; even the minimum coherent setting traces 7.625 billion rays before accounting for surfaces. Dense coherent generation belongs in an offline, benchmarked GPU job; a dense CPU onboarding run is not a credible quick check. CPU geometric precomputation and a tiny ray-wave reference subset are a practical first stage. No timing benchmark was run here.

Storage is modest after precomputation: 25 nodes × 61 bands × 25² float32 values ≈3.64 MiB per lens/stop/depth, or ≈18.2 MiB for five stops. This describes the current 25-pixel support only; converged diffraction tails can require larger variable support.

Suggested engineering acceptance thresholds below are **proposed**, not reported results or requirements from the cited authors:

- Optical calibration: verify a perfect circular pupil against Airy radius `1.22 lambda N`, optical cutoff `1/(lambda N)`, and an independent reference PSF/MTF. At 400–700 nm/f2–8, first-zero diameter ranges about 1.95–13.66 µm, so diffraction materially interacts with 3–5.76 µm sensels.
- Numerical convergence: increase pupil/ray samples, propagation sampling/padding, quadrature and kernel support independently. Require normalized PSF L1 change ≤1%, centroid change ≤0.05 native pixel, and absolute MTF difference ≤0.01 through the relevant optical/native Nyquist range; inspect sagittal/tangential directions and tails. Monte Carlo convergence needs repeated independent seeds, not only one doubled sample count.
- Support/energy: retain ≥99% of a sufficiently converged larger-support reference, or report the discarded halo and choose a stated application budget. Save pre-normalization energy; unit-sum normalization alone hides truncation and vignetting. Test impulse flux, finite-window boundary handling and uniform-field response for the spatially varying operator. Small square support cannot silently claim full Airy energy.
- Integration/order: compare one-pass optical blur → spectral response/native area integration → CFA to a high-resolution numerical reference; delta PSF, uniform source and chromatic centroid checks catch double integration and registration errors. Distortion/throughput Jacobians need their own accounting.
- Measured agreement: compare repeated RAW captures and held-out states in the **same effective measurement domain**, within calibration repeatability/uncertainty; assess PSF centroid, encircled energy, asymmetry and 2D OTF/MTF. MTF magnitude alone loses OTF phase and does not identify a unique 2D PSF.
